Agent instructions

For AI agents

Start with the tokens. Everything here is also available as plain files at this site's root.

  1. Use brand-tokens.json for every color, font, and type value. Never guess a hex code. Use semantic.* tokens for UI.
  2. Read voice-and-tone.md before writing copy, and run its review checklist before returning.
  3. Check names, dates, times, and capitalization against writing-style-guide.md.
  4. Follow brand-guide.md for logo, color, type, accessibility, and imagery.
  5. Every link and CTA points to HoustonsFirst.org. Never write “HFBC” in public copy.
  6. If a rule isn't covered, say so. Don't invent one.

Digital accessibility

For web, Rock, email, and app text and UI. Print and large display follow the printed guide.

Rules

  • No white text on Sky, Apricot, or Kelly. Use Slate text.
  • Buttons: Navy with white, or Sky with Slate.
  • Sky text only at 24px+ (19px+ bold). Links and small CTAs use Navy.
  • Body copy: Slate on white or Gray, or white on Slate or Navy.

Use

  • Campuswide logo for anything spanning more than one campus; campus logos only for single-campus audiences.
  • Clearspace: half the logomark's height/width on all sides.
  • Minimum height: primary 0.3 in (7.62 mm); stacked 0.75 in (19.05 mm).
  • Mark alone on print? “Houston's First Baptist Church” must appear on the piece.
  • Over dark backgrounds the mark may be 100% white or screened to 50%.

Never

  • Stretch, warp, skew, or slant it
  • Rearrange components or change the lettering
  • Recolor it or add effects (shadows, glows, outlines)
  • Place it on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Add elements to or around it
